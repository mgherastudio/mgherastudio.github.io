Privacy Policy

Khinkali Chef, a mobile game by Mghera. In effect from 23 November 2026.

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play the Khinkali Chef app on Android or iPhone. "We" means Mghera, the developer of the game.

  • We do not collect or share any personal data through the app.
  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or purchases, and no tracking.
  • It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • We do not run a server for the app. Your progress is not sent to us.

Data saved by the app

The app saves these on your device:

  • your current or finished serving, so you can continue it later
  • the language you chose
  • for a moment, the recipe you picked next, while the game reloads

We cannot see this data. If device backup is switched on, your phone may copy it to your own Google or Apple account and restore it when you reinstall the app or change phones.

Permissions

On Android the app can vibrate the phone and has permission to use the internet. The internet permission comes with the software the app is built on; the game does not use it to send anything about you.

Your phone and the app store

Your phone's own software and the app store you use may collect data under their own rules, such as installs, crashes or purchases. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy. App stores may show us anonymous totals, such as the number of installs and crash reports. We do not use them to identify players.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and solve the problem, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), which may process messages outside your country. We keep messages only as long as needed for that, and delete them sooner if you ask.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On your device. Game data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Copies in your phone's backup are managed in your Google or Apple account.
  • Emails. Ask us and we will delete them.

Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. No method of storing data is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, or to receive a copy. The only personal data we may hold is an email you sent us: write to us and we will act on your request. You can also complain to the data protection authority (the privacy regulator) in your country.

Children

The app is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at younger children. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

This web page

This page has no cookies, ads or analytics of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If the app's use of data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
